# How the choice of distiller affected first submissions

Lynx Cognition distilled earlier workflow demonstrations into Skills. For each matched task, the operator received the same business inputs in a fresh session. The table compares the baseline with a Skill generated by GLM-5.3 or DeepSeek Flash. Each row includes the complete cohort assigned to that comparison, including ties and failed deliveries. The two percentage rows for payment approval share the same baseline sessions. [Per-case scores and rounds](distiller-scores.csv)

| Workflow and execution surface | Matched observations per arm | Metric | No Skill | GLM Skill | DeepSeek Skill |
|---|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Finance decisions, native Chrome | 20 of 40 tickets | Mean business points / 100 | 79.75 | 100 | 100 |
| Short-payment confirmation, native Chrome | 4 tickets | Mean business points / 100 | 43.75 | 86.25 | 100 |
| Two-invoice cash application, simulated ERP | 16 remittances | Mean business points / 100 | 28.13 | 94.84 | 100 |
| Payment approval, simulated ERP, raw Skill | 16 tickets × 2 rounds | Exact first decisions | 25/32 (78.13%) | 32/32 (100%) | 30/32 (93.75%) |
| Payment approval, simulated ERP, reviewed Skill | Same 16 tickets × 2 rounds | Exact first decisions | 25/32 (78.13%) | 32/32 (100%) | 32/32 (100%) |
| Invoice reconciliation, native workflow | 1 held-out task | Accepted delivery | 1/1 | 1/1 | 1/1 |

Both distillers yielded gains with the tested operator. Their ordering varied by task and Skill version. The native finance comparison covers 12 receivables and eight payables tickets scheduled for DeepSeek. Six improved with either Skill and 14 were already fully correct. The other 20 native tickets have A/B scores only. The strict native protocol additionally checks the browser trace and cleanup. [All 40 native business and protocol results](finance.md)

In the two-invoice cohort, 0/16, 13/16 and 16/16 first deliveries were fully correct. The original 32 baseline and GLM sessions were reused. A later block added the missing 16 DeepSeek sessions with the same case inputs, precedents, operator, budget, CLI build and scorer. Timing and session variation can affect the observed difference. Each distiller supplied one archived Skill for this workflow. [All 48 first submissions, actions and environment](batches/remittance-distillers-20261119/README.md)

The payment approval comparison used one generated candidate per distiller. The raw row scores the candidates before product review. Both reviewed versions went through the DeepSeek Flash editor. Each approval needed the exact decision and all required reasons. In the invoice reconciliation task, all three produced four correct rows. That single trial per arm provides little information about differences between distillers. The results measure Skill generation with a fixed execution model. Generalization to other operators or new training demonstrations would require separate trials.

## Where the workflow fell short

* On **I-7547**, the unaided operator earned 30, GLM earned 45, and DeepSeek earned 100. The four-ticket native confirmation cohort includes this partial success and a ticket where all three scored 100. [First submissions and action histories](cases/I-7547/history.json)
* The GLM Skill left a small balance in collections on three two-invoice remittances that called for closing it. Each delivery had one fully correct row and one partially correct row. **RMT-765** scored 72.5 without a Skill and 72.5 with GLM. **RMT-786** went from 15 to 72.5 and **RMT-793** from 0 to 72.5. DeepSeek earned 100 on all three. [RMT-765](batches/remittance-distillers-20261119/RMT-765/history.json) · [RMT-786](batches/remittance-distillers-20261119/RMT-786/history.json) · [RMT-793](batches/remittance-distillers-20261119/RMT-793/history.json)
* In the first approval round, the raw DeepSeek Skill led to a release on **INV-8259** and a referral on **INV-8350**. Both payments required a hold for an unverified bank account. DeepSeek got both right in round two. Both rounds count. [Approval scores by ticket and round](distiller-scores.csv)
* **Numbers → ownCloud → Rocket.Chat** tested whether the operator could deliver a working replenishment workbook. Both the baseline and the Lynx Skill completed **1/2** scheduled attempts on the same held-out task. In the failed Skill attempt, Numbers stopped responding during formula entry and the operator delivered no workbook. In the failed baseline attempt, all 32 initial values were correct, but only 16 formulas survived export. Acceptance also required recalculation after input changes, upload, sharing and delivery. This historical A/B stress test had no paired distiller comparison. [Task and template](../cases/workbook/evaluation/task.md) · [All four trial outcomes and source digest](workbook-trials.json)
